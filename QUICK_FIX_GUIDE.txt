┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WHY THIS HAPPENED                                                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Most tutorials show sensors wired LEFT-to-RIGHT:
  s[0]=LEFT, s[5]=RIGHT
  Position: {+3, +2, +1, -1, -2, -3}

Your wiring is RIGHT-to-LEFT:
  s[0]=RIGHT, s[5]=LEFT  
  Position: {-3, -2, -1, +1, +2, +3}

The position array tells PID where each sensor is located.
If it doesn't match reality, PID calculates wrong direction!
